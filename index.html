Chad Yang

PostDoc @ University of Tübingen

Hey, thanks for stopping by! 👋 I’m from Taiwan and I’m currently a PostDoc in University of Tübingen, Germany (MHM-Lab, PI: Thomas Wolfers).

My interests lie broadly at the intersection of developing robust and reliable ML techniques, including Large-scale Self-Supervised Learning, Adversarial Attacks/Privacy, and Continuous Learning methods. I specialize in developing ML models from Multi-Modality signals, such as Electrical Physiologies (ECG/EDA/EEG), Medical Images (MRI), and Speech Processing. Before joining the MHM-Lab, I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering from National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan supervised by Prof. Chi-Chun, Lee.
